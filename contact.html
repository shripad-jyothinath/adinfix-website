What happens next

No mystery.
No 40-slide deck.

A good first conversation should leave you with more clarity than you arrived with — even if we never work together.

01 / 30 minutes

Listen

We ask about the category, the constraint, the numbers and what has already been tried. No pitch, no slides.

02 / 3–5 days

Diagnose

We come back with a short, honest read on what we would solve first and why. Sometimes that is not what you expected.

Before you write

A few practical
questions.

Do you work with businesses outside Chennai?

Yes. Chennai is home, but the work is not limited by pin code. We work with teams across India and internationally, with a mix of in-person and remote sessions.

How quickly can we start?

A growth audit usually takes two to three weeks, depending on access to data and stakeholders. Most engagements begin with that diagnostic.

What budgets do you work with?

We work with growth budgets rather than a fixed minimum. The scope is designed around the outcome, the category and the speed you need.

Will we work with the same team throughout?

Yes. The senior lead in the first conversation is the senior lead on the work. We keep the team deliberately lean so the thinking does not get handed off.

Can you work with our existing agency or in-house team?

Yes. We can lead, support or work alongside an existing team. The important thing is that someone owns the loop, not that every task sits with us.
