Before you ask

A few practical
questions.

Still unsure? Write to mail@adinfix.com and we will reply with a straight answer.

Do you work with in-house marketing teams?

Yes. We can lead the work, plug into an existing team, or operate as the extra senior bench your team needs. The model is built around clear ownership, not turf.

How quickly can we start?

A growth audit usually takes two to three weeks, depending on access to data and stakeholders. Most engagements begin with that diagnostic so the first move is based on evidence, not assumption.

Do you handle traditional media as well as digital?

Yes. Adinfix has planned and bought print, TV, FM and OOH since the 1980s. We still believe the channel should follow the audience, not the other way around.

What does success look like?

We agree on a small set of business measures before work begins: pipeline, qualified leads, revenue, retention or share of search. Reach and engagement can matter, but they are not the finish line.

What budgets do you work with?

We work with growth budgets rather than a fixed minimum. The scope is designed around the outcome, the category and the speed you need. Tell us what you are trying to achieve and we will tell you what it takes.
