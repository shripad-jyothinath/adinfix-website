The story

We started in print.
We think in outcomes.

Adinfix began in Chennai in 1983 as a full-service advertising agency. The tools have changed. The channels have multiplied. The job has not: understand the audience, find the sharpest truth, and put it in front of the right people.

Today we work across brand strategy, web and eCommerce, search, performance media, social, content, video and traditional media. One senior team, accountable for the whole loop — not a relay race between departments.

We are deliberately lean. The people in the room at the start are the people who do the work. That is how the thinking stays intact, and how the learning compounds.

What we believe

Four habits.
No theatre.

Agency values are easy to write and hard to keep. These are the four we use to decide what to do, what to stop, and who to hire.

01

Curiosity

Ask the question behind the brief. Read the category, the customer and the data before falling in love with an idea.

02

Craft

Sweat the sentence, the frame and the last 5%. Craft is not decoration; it is how trust gets built at speed.

03

Candour

Say what the work needs, not what protects the deck. Honest counsel is the most useful thing an agency can give.

04

Compounding

Make every campaign leave the brand, the data and the team stronger than it found them. Growth should accumulate.

Four decades of shifts

The market changed.
So did we.

We have watched channels arrive, fragment and reinvent themselves. The constant is a simple discipline: follow the audience, measure what matters, and keep the idea intact.

1983

The beginning

Adinfix opens in Chennai as a full-service advertising agency, built on print, ideas and relationships.

1990s

Beyond print

Television, radio and outdoor join the mix. Media planning becomes a core discipline, not an afterthought.

2000s

The digital shift

Websites, search and eCommerce enter the brief. The agency learns to build as well as broadcast.

2010s

The performance era

Paid media, analytics and conversion optimisation make accountability a daily habit, not a quarterly review.

Today

The compound era

AI-assisted search, first-party data and connected creative raise the bar. The loop is the advantage.
